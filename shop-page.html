Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding our direct origin sourcing, packaging standards, domestic delivery, and wholesale orders.

eco Where are your dried fruits, saffron, and nuts sourced from? expand_more

All our products are sourced directly from multigenerational farming families and single-estate orchards across Afghanistan (Kandahar, Herat, Kunduz, Panjshir) and Iran (Khorasan, Bam, Kashan). We work directly with local farmers under ethical trade principles with zero middleman markups.

spa Do you use sulfur dioxide or synthetic preservatives in your dried fruits? expand_more

No. Our dried fruits—including our royal green raisins, Shakarpara apricots, and white mountain figs—are 100% naturally sun-cured or shade-cured in traditional kishmish khanas. They contain zero added sulfur, zero chemical brighteners, and no artificial sweeteners.

inventory_2 How should I store dried fruits, nuts, and saffron to preserve freshness? expand_more

Store dried fruits and raw nuts in an airtight container or keep them in our resealable multi-layer nitrogen-barrier pouches in a cool, dry pantry away from direct sunlight. For prolonged shelf-life exceeding 6 months, refrigeration is recommended. Saffron threads should always be kept in their airtight tin in a dark, dry cupboard.

local_shipping What are the shipping times and costs across Australia & internationally? expand_more

We offer Complimentary Express Courier Shipping across Australia on all orders over $100 AUD (flat-rate $9.95 AUD for orders below). Orders dispatched from Sydney same-day or next business day. For international retail or air-freight commercial inquiries, please contact our logistics desk.

warehouse Do you provide wholesale, food-service, or pallet-sized B2B orders? expand_more

Yes. In addition to our retail artisan pouches, we supply 1kg vacuum packs, 5kg–10kg catering master cartons, and container-load pallet consignments for bakeries, food manufacturers, and specialty grocers complete with Australian quarantine compliance documentation and Certificates of Analysis (COA).
