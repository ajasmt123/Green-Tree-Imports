Transparency & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding our dried fruits, shipping logistics, and bulk ordering specifications.

All our dried fruits (green raisins, black raisins, Shakarpara apricots, mountain figs, and mulberries) are sourced directly from verified multi-generational farms in the historic agricultural valleys of Kandahar, Herat, and Bamyan in Afghanistan. Our saffron and barberries are procured directly from Khorasan, Iran. We work without intermediary traders to ensure origin traceability and fair grower compensation.
No. Our dried fruits undergo traditional solar dehydration or shade curing in adobe Kishmish Khana facilities. We never use artificial sulfur washes, sugar syrups, or synthetic color enhancers. Moisture content is strictly controlled via automated batch testing to preserve stability naturally.
When kept sealed in our multi-layer barrier pouches in a cool, dark pantry below 20°C, our dried fruits hold pristine freshness for 12 to 18 months. For prolonged freshness in warmer humid climates, we recommend airtight refrigerated storage.
Domestic Australian orders are fulfilled from our Sydney distribution hub via Australia Post Express or tracked courier service. We offer free shipping on retail orders over $100. International retail consignments and freight dispatches are handled with expedited air couriers with complete customs documentation.
Our pallet minimum for domestic wholesale starts at 100kg mixed units with flexible commercial terms. For full-container export contracts, our trade division assists with custom bulk 10kg/25kg carton packaging, lab certificates of analysis (COA), and freight booking.
