Harvest Provenance & Craft

Why Royal Kandahar Kishmish Stands in a Class of Its Own

Unlike industrial golden sultanas that rely on chemical sulfur smoke tanks to hold artificial color, our raisins gain their jade luster through centuries-old passive engineering in high desert valleys.

air

The Ancient Kishmish Khana

Harvested grapes are hung by hand on timber lattice rafters inside vaulted mud-brick cooling houses. Slotted vertical apertures let dry mountain winds blow across the fruit, naturally dehydrating them in pure shade over 40 days without sun-scorching.

nature Zero chemical preservatives or sulfur smoke
terrain

Arghandab River Mineral Valley

Cultivated at an altitude of 1,010 meters in Kandahar’s alluvial basin. Irrigated by crystalline Hindu Kush snowmelt and grown in calcium-rich loam that imparts an unmistakable naturally honeyed, vegetal undertone.

Kandahar Province • 31.6289° N, 65.7372° E
biotech

Lab-Verified Purity

Each shipment is independently tested for heavy metals, pesticides, and microbial purity. Cleared through the Australian Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry (DAFF) biosecurity regime.

Sommelier Sensory Report

Subtle floral sweetness with zero sticky residue

Standard commercial raisins are frequently sprayed with sunflower or mineral oils to prevent clumping. Our Royal Kandahar Green Raisins are 100% dry and tactilely clean. The palate opens with crisp honeysuckle, subtle green apple peel, and finishes with a velvety, chewy fig-like sweetness.

Pantry Storage

Keep inside an airtight pouch or glass jar in a cool, dark cupboard away from direct sunlight. Stays vibrant and chewy for up to 18 months.

kitchen

Extended Chilling

In warm tropical zones or during Australian summer months, refrigeration preserves the jade chlorophyll hue and retains maximum grape moisture.
